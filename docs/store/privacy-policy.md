# Touchline Legend — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 30 September 2026

*Touchline Legend* is an offline football management simulation developed for mobile devices.

## Overview

Your career data (manager profile, club, season progress, settings) is stored **locally on your device only**, using the Hive local database. We operate no servers and never receive your game data.

## Data we collect

**None.** The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. Nothing you do in the game leaves your device.

## Data stored on your device

Game saves (career, squad, season) and your settings are kept in app-private storage on this device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

## Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files.

## Advertising (planned)

A future release of Touchline Legend may include advertisements served by Google AdMob. When that happens, the ad provider may collect device identifiers and usage data to deliver and measure ads, as described in Google's own privacy policy. This update will be announced in the release notes and this policy will be updated **before** the ad-enabled version ships.

## Children

Touchline Legend is not directed at children under 13. The store listing targets ages 13 and over.

## Your choices

All game data is local, so you can delete everything at any time by clearing the app's storage from your device settings or by uninstalling the app.
