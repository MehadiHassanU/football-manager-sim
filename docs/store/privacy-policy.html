Touchline Legend

Your Tactics. Your Club. Your Legacy.

Privacy Policy · Effective date: 20 October 2026

Touchline Legend is an offline football management simulation developed for mobile devices.

Overview

Your career data (manager profile, club, season progress, settings) is stored locally on your device only, using the Hive local database. We operate no servers and never receive your game data.

Data we collect

None of our own. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics of ours, and no servers that receive your game data. The only third-party component that can send data off your device is the advertising SDK described below, and only when you choose to watch a rewarded video.

Data stored on your device

Game saves (career, squad, season) and your settings are kept in app-private storage on this device. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of it.

Permissions

The app requests no dangerous permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location, or files. It uses the internet only for the optional rewarded video described below.

Advertising

Touchline Legend contains one optional ad: a rewarded video served by Google AdMob, shown only when you tap "Watch an ad for fresh offers" on the Sponsorships screen. Watching it refreshes the list of sponsorship offers; closing it early, or never opening that screen, has no effect on your career — the best remaining offer is signed automatically when the season starts.

There are no banners, no pop-ups and no ads during matches.

When a video is requested, Google may collect device identifiers (including the advertising ID), approximate location derived from your IP address, and ad interaction data to deliver and measure the ad, as described in Google's privacy policy and its advertising technologies page. We receive only aggregate revenue reports from Google — never your personal data.

You can limit ad personalisation in your device's Google settings ("Ads" → "Delete advertising ID" / "Opt out of Ads Personalisation"). The game is fully playable either way.

Children

Touchline Legend is not directed at children under 13. The store listing targets ages 13 and over.

Your choices

All game data is local, so you can delete everything at any time by clearing the app's storage from your device settings or by uninstalling the app. You can also decline every rewarded video; the game never requires one.
