## Full description (4000 max)

```
Your Tactics. Your Club. Your Legacy.

Touchline Legend is a football management simulation that puts you in the
hot seat. Pick a league, choose your club, and build something that lasts
longer than your tenure.

CHOOSE YOUR PATH
Start anywhere. Select from dozens of real leagues and clubs — from
heavyweight giants with silverware expectations to underdog projects
where survival is a trophy.

LIVE MATCH DAYS
Every match plays out in about a minute. Watch goals, cards, injuries
and momentum swings unfold in real time with a live event feed. At
half-time, step into the dressing room and set your instructions for
the matches ahead.

TOTAL TACTICAL CONTROL
Playing style and tempo shape how your side takes the game to the
opposition, while formation decides who takes the field. Set your
instructions once and they carry into every match you manage.

BUILD THE SQUAD, SHAPE THE FUTURE
Scout the transfer market within your budget, negotiate fees, and keep
Financial Fair Play happy. Promote academy prospects and watch them grow
from raw potential into first-team stars — set their training focus and
the minutes they earn decide how fast they climb.

A LEAGUE THAT LIVES
Rivals spend, grow and age season after season. Youngsters break through,
veterans slow down, and the physio's table fills up with knocks, strains
and the occasional long-term blow — every squad is a story in motion.

MANAGE THE EXPECTATIONS
Board confidence, fan trust and your sack risk react to every result.
Win, and the stadium sings your name. Slip, and the directors start
taking calls.

TRACK YOUR LEGACY
Season-by-season standings, form guides, finances and club records
chart your rise — from debutant to legend of the touchline.

──────────────────────────────
• Offline playable — no account, no sign-in, your data stays on your device
• Clean, modern dark interface designed for one-handed play
• Free to play

Download Touchline Legend and take your seat in the dugout.
```
